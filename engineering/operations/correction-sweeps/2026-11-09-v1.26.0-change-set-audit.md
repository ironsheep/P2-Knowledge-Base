## Findings fixed (by class)

- **High.** `hub.yaml` stated the slice rule as "the lower 3 bits of its address" against "every 8th long" (Silicon Doc 2999, datasheet 718): restored as byte-address bits [4:2] with the 8-row table, the Hardware Manual wording named. `hub.yaml`'s certification row claimed one entry read: the file was re-certified whole and the row replaced. A PASM2 block in `conditional_execution.yaml` had been reclassified not-code with a false reason: back to fragment, compiled.
- **Bench results beyond their arm.** EF-109 (SKIP pattern only, absolute forms, cog), EF-112 (the 16-instruction window, TRGINT1 only), EF-117 (empty GETQX directly after a QMUL read), EF-121 (cog RAM only), EF-122 (one pattern), EF-037 (its counts and window), EF-057, EF-071 (E6 narrowed to %00001), EF-020 (WAITXMT outside the measured family, now Not documented).
- **Code that did not do what its text says.** Cog-ID and lock-ID variables tested with `>= 0` while VAR starts at zero (stopped cog 0 or returned lock 0): `object_archetypes`, `cogstop`, `spin2_cog_management`, `locknew`, `lockret`, now ID + 1. Spin2 `rdpin()` returns C in bit 31: counter and ADC examples masked or read with inline PASM2, one home for the rule in `smart_pins.yaml`. A smart-pin array store used ALTS where it needed ALTD (`smart-pin-10001`). A lock timeout compared CT unsigned (`locks.yaml`, now ADDCT1/JNCT1). Interrupt examples with no main loop ran into their ISRs. A conditional breakpoint that broke at most once. GETMS deadlines that failed at the counter wrap. A WAITCT overhead pattern that shortened every period. A period measurement routed to the wrong pin. An example that ran on into `res`.
- **Wrong facts.** The Spin2 task-pointer mapping: task n is at $11F−n (the audit's finding said $100+n; the interpreter shows the inverse). SUMx overflow: C is the correct sign (the release had marked it not documented). `labels.yaml` `#label` relative only within one domain (CP-052). XZERO's 1/3 is `$2AAA_AAAA+1` (E-032 filed). Async WYPIN words are 1 to 32 bits. `fblock` fixed timing. Crystal wait `/100` restored, with Parallax's `/200` named. COGID/COGSTOP field width given both ways. SAR's unsigned caveat restored with the manual's contrary sentence named.
- **Contradicted documents not named.** ORG's two default limits, LOCKREL `00L`/`C0L`, Z[15:0] vs Z, SKIPF ILLEGAL, the 5ms crystal wait.
- **Sourced material removed without a reason, restored.** The DRV/FLT two-instruction equivalence (now in all 16 files, each from its own manual line), the DIR-bit meaning, DECOD/ENCOD, SETS "unlike ALTx", GETPTR in RFVAR/RFVARS, the no-dead-band PWM fact and its pointer, the cog-fill negative, the QMUL REP mitigation (as a pointer), the LUT register form, the `$FFFE` largest terminating count, hub single-access atomicity.
- **Class sweeps.** Erratum E-numbers removed from served keys and aliases everywhere except the errata page. "Not used" and "not documented here" forms. Timing labels "cog or LUT exec / hub exec". Bare `related:` names to full paths (none dropped). Attention flag set by a cog's own COGATN (EF-099). "Selectable event" wording. One `##Label` sentence across the 47 S-branch files.
- **Fix-pass audit.** `hub.yaml` RDLONG read the CSV's execution-mode columns as data location: corrected. `hubset.yaml` names Parallax's `/200`. The ADC own-group statement (three ADC files and `pin-power-domains.yaml`) now says in its source that it is two datasheet statements read together.

## Removal reasons

Removed claims had no source line, CP or EF, or were derived (formulas, counts, bandwidth figures), or were advice; each file's certification row names them. Two judged removals: the lock-order deadlock anti-pattern (no ingested source), and "COGSTOP leaks a lock number" (no source; now Not documented, bench question VO-J-056). No `related:` entry was removed without a redirect.

## Arbiter rulings

- SUMx: correct sign, from the CSV (reverses the release's earlier rows).
- ADC VIO/GIO reads its own four-pin group: kept, basis stated.
- Lock number after COGSTOP: Not documented until VO-J-056 runs.

## Pre-flight gates

After the fix pass the full gates ran on a throwaway working-tree index. They caught what the fixers' scoped checks had not: stale compile fingerprints (refreshed), nine citations in a wrong form and two unsourced quantities (fixed in form, claims unchanged), the new `colorspace-and-dvi.yaml` not reached by the term "streamer" (aliases added), and five invented citation keys (`encoding_source`, `alignment_source`, `single_access_source`, `spin2_source`, `timing_functions_source`) whose citations would have shipped, because the delivery filter strips only the standard source keys; each merged into or renamed to `source:`/`sources:`. Remaining before the content commit: Index Integrity reports the four new files absent at HEAD, which the commit resolves; `--post-commit` re-runs it.
