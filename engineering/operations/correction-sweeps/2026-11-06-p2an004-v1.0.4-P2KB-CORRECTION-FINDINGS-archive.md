### F-638 — P2AN004 says `P_LOW_150K` "adds a soft pull that rejects noise on an open input" — `RESOLVED` (found 2026-11-06, the F-356 sweep; released P2AN004 v1.0.4, 2026-11-06)
`P2AN004.md:147`, `:188` and `examples-library/light-to-freq-reader.spin2:34`, on `TIK_MODE = P_COUNTER_TICKS | P_LOW_150K`. Silicon Doc, `%TT`, all smart pin modes: "x0 = output disabled, regardless of DIR". `TIK_MODE` carries no `P_OE`, and a drive strength acts only while the pin drives, so this setting gives no pull. The companion does not carry the claim.
> **Applied 2026-11-06, P2AN004 v1.0.4:** the constant and its comment are out of the listing and the example file, and the Verify line follows Stephen's three cases: a pull-down belongs to a line that can float, not to a driven input. A search for a drive strength on a smart-pin mode word with no `P_OE` across the masters, example libraries and the KB finds no other site. Removing the setting changes nothing electrically by the Silicon Doc's rule; no P2 run was made. The TSL235R's output type is in no ingested source, so the note does not state it.
> **Closed on the released PDF, 2026-11-06:** `P_LOW_150K` and "soft pull" are absent; `TIK_MODE = P_COUNTER_TICKS` prints on p7 and the Verify line on p8.
