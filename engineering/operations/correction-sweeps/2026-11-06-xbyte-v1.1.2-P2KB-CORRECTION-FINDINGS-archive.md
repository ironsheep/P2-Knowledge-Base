### F-627 — XBYTE Guide §5.3 never gives the variable-length byte format or names `FVAR`/`FVARS` — `RESOLVED` (found 2026-11-05, «#440»; released XBYTE v1.1.2, 2026-11-06)
`xbyte-body.md:410` introduces the instructions by extension only, and neither the guide nor its examples library mentions the directives that emit the format. Fix: add the layout and the directives to §5.3.
> **Applied 2026-11-05, XBYTE v1.1.2 prepared:** §5.3 carries the layout table, the two overrides, a compiled example and the signed/unsigned pairing.
> **Closed on the released PDF, 2026-11-06:** read on p32. A fresh `pnut-ts` compile gives `FVAR 300` as `AC 02`, `FVAR 7` as `07`, `FVARS 63` as `3F`, `FVARS -64` as `40`.

### F-628 — XBYTE Guide §15.4 says "the assembler emits" a two-byte offset without saying how — `RESOLVED` (found 2026-11-05, «#440»; released XBYTE v1.1.2, 2026-11-06)
`xbyte-body.md:1514`: the -64..+63 range is right; the sentence omits that `FVARS` is what makes the assembler size the value. Fix: name `FVARS`.
> **Applied 2026-11-05, XBYTE v1.1.2 prepared:** widened on a compile. "Let it compute the value" was itself wrong for this program: `FVARS` takes no label defined later in the file (CP-027), and the guide's offsets are measured from a label after the operand. §15.4 now names `FVARS`, gives `FVARS -200` as `$B8 $7E`, and states that limit.
> **Closed on the released PDF, 2026-11-06:** read on p77. A fresh `pnut-ts` compile gives `FVARS -200` as `B8 7E`, and `FVARS` on a label defined later stops with `Undefined symbol`.
