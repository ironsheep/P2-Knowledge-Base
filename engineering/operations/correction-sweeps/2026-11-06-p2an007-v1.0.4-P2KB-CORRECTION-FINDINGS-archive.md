## Found during the 2026-10 manual releases, the register reconciliation and the pre-release audit — F-565

### F-565 — `keywords/STRUCT.yaml` `gotchas.struct_pointer_obj_returns` said `pCfg := sensor.getConfig()` is a COMPILE ERROR for structures over 15 longs; no ingested source and no compile-proof record states it — `RESOLVED` (found 2026-10-09, «#415»; KB released v1.25.2; released P2AN007 v1.0.4, 2026-11-06)
> **Applied 2026-11-06, P2AN007 v1.0.4:** lines 57, 406 and 422 say what `CP-028` and the companion say; `BYTEMOVE` is out.
> **Answered 2026-11-06, CP-028:** the compile probe needed no bench. The bare form is rejected at 5 bytes or more, in a child object and in the same object, with `Expected ","` up to 15 longs and `Expected a variable (m251)` from 16; 4 bytes or fewer compiles; the bracket form always compiles. So the removed claim was wrong in scope, and the replacement "writes through the pointer" was wrong from 5 bytes. `STRUCT.yaml`, `typed-pointers.yaml` and the P2AN007 companion now say this. "Larger uses BYTEMOVE" goes from `STRUCT.yaml` and the companion: Spin2 v55 states copy and swap at any size and no BYTEMOVE.
> **Closed on the released PDF, 2026-11-06:** "of 5 bytes or more" prints on p14 and "copy and swap work at any size" on p3; `BYTEMOVE` and "larger than 15 longs" are absent.
Found 2026-10-09 by «#415» fixing `language/spin2/keywords/STRUCT.yaml`. The page carried a `wrong:`/`correct:` pair, the explanation that "the compiler may interpret the return as the full structure", and the scope "only across OBJ boundaries". The only origin was the upstream lead `external-inputs/pnut_ts_facts/Pointer-Usage-Guide.md` (not an authority). The same pair sat in `operators/typed-pointers.yaml`, and «#416» applied the same treatment there on 2026-10-10.
