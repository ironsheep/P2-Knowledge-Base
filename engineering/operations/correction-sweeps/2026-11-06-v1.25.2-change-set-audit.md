## Findings fixed

Each is in the commits of the range. The reasons that matter for removals:

- **`event_interrupt_config.yaml`:** `debug_use` on NIXINT goes because NIXINT acts on INT1, INT2 and INT3 only;
  "Execution mode" goes from the saved context because the INTERRUPTS section saves the return address and flags.
- **`interrupts.yaml`:** "Prevents immediate re-triggering" goes with no replacement, and no source states it.
  "Blocks other COGs" goes because each cog has its own hub slot.
- **`STRUCT.yaml`, P2AN007 companion:** "uses BYTEMOVE" goes. Spin2 v55 states copy and swap at any size and never
  ties BYTEMOVE to structure assignment. "Only across OBJ boundaries" goes because the same-object form fails too.
- **`pasm2-getting-started.yaml`:** `combinations_are_compiler_verified` goes; its caveat moves to `how_to_declare`.
- **P2AN003 companion:** "leaves the first output cycle undefined" and "produces incorrect dithering" go. The source
  says only that Y is captured during reset and that the period must be a multiple of 256.

## Left as it stands, with the reason

- **Generic advice** in `interrupts.yaml` and `event_interrupt_config.yaml` ("Keep ISRs short", "Use polling for
  non-critical events"): guidance with no P2 fact in it. Stephen: "we end up removing useful things."
- **`cordic_solver.yaml` (PASM2 and Spin2):** these now carry v55's sentence "accommodates interrupts and only stalls
  them briefly". v55 says that about user interrupts generally and not about the CORDIC window. That Spin2's `*`, `/`
  and `SQRT` use the CORDIC has no v55 line either.
- **`entry_overhead` in `interrupts.yaml`:** CALLD's own timings, and its note now says so.
- **`p2an003` "valid only from the 2nd period":** not traced in this audit.
