P2 HD Audio Add-on Set (#64014)




The P2 HD Audio Add-On Set comprises a 4-channel 32 bit 192 k Audio ADC (Analog to Digital
Converter) and a 2-channel 16-bit 18-990 ohm Audio DAC (Digital to Analog Converter).

This add-on set is supplied as a kit including both the ADC and DAC boards. Each board is
compatible with any standard single Parallax P2 accessory socket found on most P2
development boards, and are designed to fit side-by-side if installed at a dual port. The
connector is a standard 2x6-way 0.1” pitch socket, and the boards could be used with a wide
range of microcontrollers that use 3.3 V signal levels.

The ADC has 4 input channels available via two stereo 3.5 mm jack sockets, or at two sets of
unpopulated 0.1” pitch 3-way header pads.

The DAC has 2 output channels available via one stereo 3.5 mm jack socket, or unpopulated
0.1” pitch 3-way header pads.

The jack sockets can be used in mono or stereo modes, to allow the left and right channels to
carry independent signals, or to be part of a stereo pair.

The P2 HD Audio Add-on Set extends the high quality audio capabilities of the Parallax P2
multicore microcontroller Smart Pins into the real world, and enables advanced sound
capabilities, processing and effects commonly used in robotics, interactive displays,
conferencing, stadium, studio and industrial applications.
